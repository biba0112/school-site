How to play

  1. Choose a game in the arcade. Each game is short (2–3 minutes) and teaches one idea.
  2. Answer before the time bar runs out. A right answer gives 100 points + a speed bonus.
  3. Three right in a row starts a 🔥 streak: double points until you make a mistake.
  4. At the end you win a medal (🥇 90 % right · 🥈 70 % · 🥉 50 %) and read the 🧠 Remember box.
  5. Play all 5 games to unlock the 🏆 Final challenge, then print your 🏅 certificate. Your best scores are saved in this browser.
  6. 📖 Key words (top right) lists every word with its meaning.

📖 Key words

Stamina · endurance · strength · flexibility · heart rate (bpm) · pulse: count 15 s × 4.
