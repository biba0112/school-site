    The chemistry

    • Atom: the smallest particle of an element.
    • Element: only one type of atom. Compound: two or more different atoms chemically joined. Mixture: two or more substances that are not chemically joined.
    • Physical change: no new substance; easy to undo (melting, dissolving, mixing). Chemical change: a new substance is made; hard to undo (burning, cooking, rusting).

    The particle pictures are a model: atoms are not really coloured balls. Touching atoms = chemically joined.
