    The chemistry

    • pH scale: below 7 = acid · 7 = neutral · above 7 = alkali. The further from 7, the more acidic or alkaline.
    • Indicator: a substance that changes colour in acids and alkalis. Universal indicator shows the pH number.
    • Concentrated: lots of acid (or alkali) in a little water. Dilute: a little acid (or alkali) in lots of water. Adding water moves the pH closer to 7, but never past 7.
    • Neutralisation: acid + alkali → salt + water. The pH moves to 7.

    The particle pictures are a model, not to scale: real liquids have far more particles. Marie's pH numbers are rounded.

    🥽 Safety rules in the lab

    • Wear eye protection the whole time.
    • Wear a lab apron (or lab coat) to protect your clothes and skin.
    • Tie long hair back, away from chemicals and flames.
    • 🚫Never taste or smell chemicals. Never touch them with your fingers.
    • Corrosive: concentrated acids and alkalis (like oven cleaner) can burn skin and eyes.
    • Flammable: alcohol catches fire easily. Keep it away from flames.
    • 💧Add drops slowly with a pipette. Use only small amounts.
    • 🚿On your skin or in your eyes? Rinse with lots of cold water and tell your teacher.
    • 🧻Wipe up spills straight away and tell your teacher.
    • 🧼Wash your hands after the lab.
