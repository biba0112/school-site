        How to use this simulation

        1. Choose an acid and an alkali on the left. The acids from our lessons come first; the challenge acids are extra.
        2. Predict the name of the salt. Then drag each piece (or tap a piece, then tap a box) to where it ends up: the salt or the water. Or press React! and watch the four pieces swap partners. ↺ Put it back undoes the reaction.
        3. Under the reaction you see the word equation and, for interest, the equation with formulas (in Grade 7 you need the word equation).
        4. How to name the salt (right) explains every step. The rule box lights up the rule for your acid.
        5. Tasks (right): three sets — Pre-lab, During the lab and Homework. ⚙ Set it up for me picks the chemicals. Write your explanations on your worksheet.
        6. Quiz: drag a word into each box to name the salt, then build the products from the pieces.
        7. Build the formula: 8 reactions. Sometimes you build the salt and the water, sometimes you work backwards and build the acid and the alkali. The small numbers in the formulas are added for you.
        8. Wrong answer? The first time you get a tip. The second time the simulation shows the answer. Stars: right first time ★★★ · right after a tip ★★ · answer shown: no stars.

        The chemistry

        • Neutralisation: acid + alkali → salt + water.
        • The H from the acid and the OH from the alkali join to make water, H2O.
        • The salt's 1st name is the metal from the alkali. Its 2nd name comes from the acid: hydro…ic acid → …ide · …ic acid → …ate · …ous acid → …ite.
        • An alkali is a base that dissolves in water. Magnesium hydroxide hardly dissolves, so it is a base, not an alkali — but it neutralises acids in the same way.

        The pieces are a model: they show which parts of the acid and the alkali end up in the salt and in the water. They are not drawn to scale.

        Remember: acid + alkali → salt + water. The salt's first name is always the metal, and the second name comes from the acid.
