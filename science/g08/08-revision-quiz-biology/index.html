How to use this revision quiz

  1. Marie is your guide. 📖 Marie's guide (top right) shows you how to revise.
  2. 1 · Study guide: 8 topic cards. Open each card, read the key ideas, look at the diagram and the red common mistake. Tap a word with a dotted line to see what it means. Practise this topic → gives you questions on that card.
  3. 2 · Test yourself: choose 🟢 Easy (know it: multiple choice), 🟠 Medium (use it: label diagrams, fill tables, sort and order cards) or 🔴 Hard (think it: tables, graphs and experiments). 10 questions per round.
  4. Drag and drop: drag a card to its place, or tap the card and then tap the place.
  5. Wrong answer? The first time you get a tip. The second time the answer is shown. Stars: right first time ★★★ · right after a tip ★★ · answer shown: no stars.
  6. Win 4 badges (📖 all 8 cards · ★ 24 or more in an Easy, a Medium and a Hard round) and print your 3 · 🏅 Certificate. Your progress is saved in this browser.

The questions come from the Unit 1 revision tests. The drawings are models, not to scale.
