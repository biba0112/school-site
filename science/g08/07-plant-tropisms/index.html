                      The biology

                      • A tropism is a growth response of a plant to a stimulus that comes from one direction. Towards the stimulus = positive, away = negative.
                      • Auxin is a plant hormone made in the tips of shoots and roots. It moves to the shaded side (or the lower side). In shoots it makes cells stretch more; in roots it slows growth.

                      The drawings are models, not to scale. Growth is shown in time-lapse: real bending takes hours to days.

                      Tropism = growth towards (+) or away from (−) a stimulus from one direction. Auxin moves to the shaded or lower side.
