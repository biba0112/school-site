            How to use this simulation

            1. Marie is your guide. 📖 Marie's guide (top right) explains all the key words.
            2. 1 · The equation: build the word equation, count the atoms in the symbol equation and watch the atoms join up in a new way.
            3. 2 · Ingredients' journey: follow water, carbon dioxide and light to the chloroplasts, label the plant and sort what goes in and out.
            4. 3 · Uses of glucose: see where the glucose goes, match each use and solve 5 glucose puzzles.
            5. 4 · Colour of light: split white light with a prism, see which colours a leaf absorbs and grow plants under coloured filters.
            6. 5 · Starch test: test a leaf for starch, put the steps in order and do four classic experiments.
            7. Animations: 👣 Step by step plays one step at a time: read it, then press Next step ▶. ↺ Replay step shows the same step again. ▶ Play all plays everything, with a stop after each step (⏸ Pause to stop).
            8. ✅ Check (in tabs 1–5) has 3 quick questions. 6 · Test yourself mixes everything (12 questions).
            9. Wrong answer? The first time you get a tip. The second time the simulation shows the answer. Stars: right first time ★★★ · right after a tip ★★ · answer shown: no stars. Win 5 badges and print your 7 · 🏅 Certificate. Your progress is saved in this browser.

            The biology

            • Photosynthesis: plants make glucose from carbon dioxide and water, using light energy absorbed by chlorophyll. Oxygen is given off.
            • carbon dioxide + water → glucose + oxygen  ·  6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂

            The drawings are models, not to scale. The plant heights in the filter garden are typical results, not real measurements.
