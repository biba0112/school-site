            The biology

            • The endocrine system is made of glands that release hormones (chemical messengers) into the blood. Hormones act only on their target organs.
            • Homeostasis keeps the inside of the body constant (temperature, water, blood glucose…) by negative feedback.

            The drawings are models, not to scale. The blood glucose numbers are typical values, not a real patient's.

            Gland → hormone → blood → target organ → response. Homeostasis keeps the inside of the body steady by negative feedback.
