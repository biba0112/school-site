                The biology

                • A sense organ is a group of receptor cells that respond to one kind of stimulus: eye (light), ear (sound, movement of the head), nose and tongue (chemicals), skin (touch, pressure, pain, temperature).
                • Receptors change the stimulus into electrical impulses. Sensory neurones carry them to the brain (the CNS), which works out what they mean.

                The drawings are models, not to scale.

                Stimulus → receptor (in a sense organ) → sensory neurone → brain → the brain works out what it means.
