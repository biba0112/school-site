How to use this simulation

Scientists answer questions with experiments. The scientific method is the list of steps they follow. You write the steps in a lab report.

    1. 1 · Experiments: choose a research question – 🎾 bouncing ball · 🚗 toy car on a ramp · 🔥 metal rods · 🧊 ice on different materials · ☀️ flask colour. Do the 13 steps in order. At the end you get 📄 My lab report to print. Then try another experiment!
    2. 2 · Practice rooms: 5 rooms: 🕵️ Variables detective · ⚖️ Judge the scientist · 📈 Graph doctor · 🔎 Evaluator · 🧮 Data analyst (work with results tables).
    3. 3 · 🏅 Certificate: type your name and print your certificate. 10 badges: one for each experiment and one for each room.
    4. Drag a word or card to a gap or a box – or tap it, then tap the place.
    5. Uncertainty = range ÷ 2. Write a result as average ± uncertainty, e.g. 11.0 ± 2.0 cm. On a graph it is an error bar: a line from average − uncertainty to average + uncertainty.
    6. Wrong answer? The first time you get a tip. The second time you see the answer. Stars: right first time ★★★ · after a tip ★★ · answer shown: no stars. Your progress is saved in this browser.

    🥽 Safety rules for experiments

    • 👂Listen to your teacher and read the method before you start.
    • 🧹Keep the floor and bench clear: bags and coats away, so nobody trips.
    • 🪑Never stand on chairs or tables. Use a clamp stand to hold things up high.
    • 🚶Walk in the lab. Never throw things.
    • 🥽Wear eye protection when there are chemicals, hot liquids or things that could fly up.
    • 🧻Clean up spills straight away and tell your teacher about any accident.
    • 🧼Wash your hands after the experiment.
