      The biology

      • Stimulus → receptor → sensory neurone → relay neurone (in the CNS) → motor neurone → effector → response.
      • CNS (central nervous system) = the brain and the spinal cord. Nerves (bundles of neurones) link the CNS to the rest of the body.
      • Effectors are muscles (they contract) and glands (they release a substance, such as saliva or a hormone).

      The drawings are models, not to scale. A real synapse is only about 20 nm (0.000 02 mm) wide.
