How to use this simulation

Heat (thermal energy) always moves from something hotter to something colder. It can move in 3 ways: conduction, convection and radiation. And temperature is not the same as heat – you will see why!

  1. 1 · 🔬 Free lab – our class experiments: 1 ♨️ Heat or temperature? (2 parts) · 2 ⭐ the metal star · 3 🌀 the convection tube · 4 ☀️ the bottles and 🔲 the Leslie cube · 5 🍦 the ice lolly picnic (insulation). Choose one and press ▶ Play; use ⏸ Pause, 🐢 Slow motion and ↺ Replay.
  2. 🔬 The particle model shows what happens inside: blue = cold, red = hot. Faster particles = higher temperature.
  3. Drag a word into a gap – or tap it, then tap the gap.
  4. Wrong answer? The first time you get a tip. The second time you see the answer. Stars: right first time ★★★ · after a tip ★★ · answer shown: no stars.
  5. Words with a dotted line – tap them to see what they mean. 🗣 Read aloud reads Marie's words for you. 🔈 reads one question.
  6. 2 · 🧠 Quiz: 17 questions about heat in real life. You can take it again – your best score counts.
