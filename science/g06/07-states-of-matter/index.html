How to use this simulation

Everything is made of tiny particles. In a solid, a liquid and a gas the particles are the same – only how close together they are and how they move is different. When you heat something, its particles get more kinetic energy (they move faster) – and it can change state!

  1. The particle jar: hold the slider to the right to heat 🔥, to the left to cool ❄️ – or use the ▲ Heat and ▼ Cool buttons (tap = a little, hold = more). Let go and it stops. Watch the thermometer (°C), the particles, the ⚡ kinetic energy bar and the 📦 space used bar. 🔧 Push the lid to try to squash it – the pressure gauge beside the jar shows the pressure of the gas – and 🫙 Pour it into another jar.
  2. Substances: 💧 water, 🍫 chocolate, 🕯️ candle wax and 🔩 iron. Jump to 🧊 Solid, 💧 Liquid or 💨 Gas with one tap.
  3. The change-of-state diagram on the right lights up: melting, freezing, evaporating / boiling, condensing.
  4. Drag a word or card – or tap it, then tap where it goes.
  5. Wrong answer? The first time you get a tip. The second time you see the answer. Stars: right first time ★★★ · after a tip ★★ · answer shown: no stars.
  6. Words with a dotted line – tap them to see what they mean.
  7. 2 · 🧠 Quiz: 15 questions about real life. 3 · 🏅 Certificate: type your name and print it. Your progress is saved in this browser.
