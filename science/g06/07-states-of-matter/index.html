How to use this simulation

Everything is made of tiny particles. In a solid, a liquid and a gas the particles are the same – only how close together they are and how they move is different. When you heat something, its particles get more kinetic energy (they move faster) – and it can change state!

  1. The particle jar: hold the slider to the right to heat 🔥, to the left to cool ❄️. Let go and it stops. Watch the thermometer (°C), the particles, the ⚡ kinetic energy bar and the 📦 space used bar. 🔧 Push the lid to try to squash it, 🫙 Pour it into another jar.
  2. 1 · Parts (4 parts): 1 🧊 Solid, liquid, gas · 2 🍫 Melting and freezing · 3 ♨️ Evaporating, boiling and condensing · 4 🔩 Same rules, different substances. The next part opens when you finish the one before.
  3. In each part: 1 🤔 Predict → 2 🧪 Do (missions – they tick themselves, the table fills itself) → 3 ✍️ Explain (4 questions) → 4 🔍 Explore.
  4. Drag a word or card – or tap it, then tap where it goes.
  5. Wrong answer? The first time you get a tip. The second time you see the answer. Stars: right first time ★★★ · after a tip ★★ · answer shown: no stars.
  6. Words with a dotted line – tap them to see what they mean. 🗣 Read aloud reads Marie's words for you. 🔈 reads one question.
  7. 2 · 🔬 Free lab: all 4 substances, plus Solid / Liquid / Gas buttons. Try anything!
  8. 3 · 🧠 Quiz: 15 questions about real life. 4 · 🏅 Certificate: type your name and print it. Your progress is saved in this browser.
