How to use this simulation

Energy makes things happen. We measure it in joules (J). There are 9 forms of energy (the coloured buttons at the top – tap one to see what it means).

⚖️ The law of conservation of energy: energy cannot be made or destroyed. It only changes from one form to another. The total always stays the same.

  1. 1 · 🔬 Free lab – 6 scenes: 🫖 kettle · 🔦 torch · 🤖 wind-up robot · 🎾 bouncing ball · 🛹 skateboarder · ☀️ Sun → apple → runner. Choose a scene and press ▶ Play; use ⏸ Pause, 🐢 Slow motion and ↺ Replay. Watch the bars change – and the TOTAL. Tap a bar to see what it means.
  2. Drag a tile into a gap – or tap it, then tap the gap.
  3. Wrong answer? The first time you get a tip. The second time you see the answer. Stars: right first time ★★★ · after a tip ★★ · answer shown: no stars.
  4. Words with a dotted line – tap them to see what they mean. 🗣 Read aloud reads Marie's words for you. 🔈 reads one question.
  5. 2 · 🧠 Quiz: 15 questions about energy in real life, at home and at school. You can take it again – your best score counts.
  6. 3 · 🏅 Certificate: type your name and print it. You win the Quiz champion badge when you finish the quiz. Your progress is saved in this browser.
