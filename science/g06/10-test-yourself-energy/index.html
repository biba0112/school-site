How to use Test yourself

20 questions about the whole Thinking about Energy unit: types of energy, energy transfers, useful and wasted energy, heat and temperature, states of matter, heat transfer, energy resources and how electricity is made. Use it to get ready for your unit test.

  1. True or false and Choose the answer: tap your answer.
  2. Sort them, Match them, Energy chain and Put in order: drag a card into a box or a gap – or tap it, then tap the place. Then press ✔ Check.
  3. Wrong answer? The first time you get a tip. The second time you see the answer. Stars: right first time ★★★ · after a tip ★★ · answer shown: no stars.
  4. Words with a dotted line – and the key words at the top – tap them to see what they mean.
  5. At the end you see your stars and the topics to revise. You can test yourself again – your best score counts.
  6. 2 · 🏅 Certificate: type your name and print it. Your progress is saved in this browser.
