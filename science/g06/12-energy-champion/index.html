⚡ Energy Champion

Hello, I'm Marie! Answer 15 energy questions in a row and climb the ladder to 1 000 000 ★. Can you become the Energy Champion?

How many teams?

1 team = the whole class plays together. With 2–4 teams, each team plays its own game with new questions, one after the other.

🙋 Ask the class

Hands up for each answer! Type the number of hands, or use − and +.

? How to play

  1. Read the question aloud. The class (or team) talks and chooses one answer.
  2. Click that answer (or press A, B, C, D). Marie asks: Final answer? Click ✔ Final answer (or press Enter).
  3. Right: climb one step. Wrong: the game ends and you keep the ★ of the last 🛟 safe step (question 5 = 1 000 ★, question 10 = 32 000 ★).
  4. Not sure? 🚶 Walk away and keep the ★ you already have.
  5. Three helpers, once each per game: ✂️ 50:50 takes away two wrong answers · 🙋 Ask the class counts hands up · 💡 Hint gives a clue from Marie.
  6. Tap a dotted word to see what it means.
  7. Every new game takes new questions from a bank of 64 (no circuits, no Sankey diagrams).
