How to use this simulation

An energy resource is something in nature that we get energy from, like coal, the wind or the Sun. Some will run out one day. Others are replaced by nature again and again.

♻️ Renewable energy resources are unlimited and can be used over and over again. Non-renewable energy resources will eventually run out.

  1. 1 · 🔬 Free lab – 10 resources: 4 non-renewable (coal, oil, natural gas, nuclear) and 6 renewable (solar, wind, hydro-electric, tidal, wave, geothermal). Tap one under the picture. 👣 Step by step plays one step at a time: read Marie, then press Next step ▶. ↺ Replay step shows the same step again. ▶ Play all plays everything, with a stop after each step (⏸ Pause to stop). One step shows ⚡ how a power station makes electricity. Watch the readings on the right: how much is left in nature, the CO₂ it makes, and whether it is working now.
  2. Drag a tile into a gap or a box – or tap it, then tap the place.
  3. Wrong answer? The first time you get a tip. The second time you see the answer. Stars: right first time ★★★ · after a tip ★★ · answer shown: no stars.
  4. Words with a dotted line – and the key words at the top – tap them to see what they mean.
  5. 2 · 🧠 Quiz: 15 questions about energy resources in real life. Tap a number to go to any question. You can take it again – your best score counts.
  6. 3 · 🏅 Certificate: type your name and print it. You win the Quiz champion badge when you finish the quiz. Your progress is saved in this browser.
